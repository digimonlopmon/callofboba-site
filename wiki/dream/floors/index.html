    关卡Floors

    梦境共有 11 层，每晚进入下一层。

    Dreamland has 11 floors; each night takes you one floor further.

    • 打完 1-6 后，下一晚回到 1-1，卡片和弹药重置；之后一直在 1-1 到 1-6 之间循环。After 1-6 the next night starts again at 1-1 with cards and ammo reset; from then on the floors 1-1 to 1-6 repeat. 来自游戏代码From the game's code
    • 在梦里倒下时：When you fall in the dream:

    卡片与弹药已重置，回到本阶段第1层。原料与梦境精华会保留。

    Cards and ammo reset, back to floor 1 of this stage. Ingredients and Dream Essence are kept.

    楼层号就是游戏书本地图页「梦境层数」显示的号码。

    Floor numbers are the ones the dream book's map page shows as "Dream Floor".
