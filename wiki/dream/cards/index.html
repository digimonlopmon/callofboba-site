    技能卡Skill Cards

    共 35 张，每张最高 5 级。清空一个房间后有 50% 几率出现三选一。

    35 cards, each up to level 5. When a room is cleared, a pick-1-of-3 appears with a 50% chance.

    清空房间，就有机会三选一。

    Clear rooms for a chance to pick 1 of 3.

    • 卡片和弹药在梦里倒下时、以及打完 1-6 之后重置。Cards and ammo reset when you fall in the dream and after floor 1-6. 来自游戏代码From the game's code
    • 每级效果是游戏卡面上的文字，数值是该等级的总量。Level effects are the game's own card text; values are the total at that level.
