    梦境Dreamland

    入睡后进入的梦境：用奶茶原料当弹药战斗，打倒敌人可能掉落梦境精华和原料。

    The dream you enter after going to sleep: you fight with drink ingredients as ammo, and defeated enemies may drop Dream Essence and ingredients.

    战斗说明:
    啵啵在梦境中，曾经恐惧的怪物游荡其中，随机掉落奶茶原料。
    探索地图，找到出口或击败首领怪物，帮助啵啵逃离梦境，收集更多原料吧!

    Battle Instructions:
    Bobo is in a dream world where monsters he once feared roam, randomly dropping ingredients for boba tea.
    Explore the map, find the exit or defeat the boss to help Bobo escape the dream and collect more ingredients!

    进入梦境Entering the dream

    在自己房间的床上互动，回答「想上床睡觉来结束这一天吗？」选「是」：这一天结束，进入梦境。

    Use the bed in your room and answer "Go to bed and end the day?" with "Yes": the day ends and you enter the dream.

    到凌晨 2:00 还没睡会昏倒，同样进入梦境。

    If you are still up at 2:00 at night you pass out, which also takes you into the dream.

    体力耗尽也会昏倒，同样进入梦境。

    Running out of stamina also makes you pass out, and that takes you into the dream too.

    台灯：跳过梦境The lamp: skipping the dream

    从第 4 天起可以开关房间里的台灯。台灯亮着时入睡不进入梦境。第 4 天早上游戏里会出现这段说明：

    From day 4 the lamp in your room can be switched on and off. Going to sleep while it is lit skips the dream. The game shows this on the morning of day 4:

    关闭台灯后入睡，将会进入梦境战斗。
    这是一盏神奇的灯。点亮它，即可避免夜晚进入梦境参与战斗。

    If you turn off the lamp and fall asleep, you will enter a Dreamland battle.
    This is a magical lamp. Lighting it will prevent you from entering the Dreamland and fighting at night.

    离开梦境Leaving the dream

    找到出口或打倒首领就能离开梦境。

    Find the exit or defeat the boss to leave the dream.

    每晚一层，顺序见关卡。

    One floor per night; see Floors for the order.

    在梦里倒下时：

    When you fall in the dream:

    卡片与弹药已重置，回到本阶段第1层。原料与梦境精华会保留。

    Cards and ammo reset, back to floor 1 of this stage. Ingredients and Dream Essence are kept.

    掉落Drops

    每打倒一个敌人：

    Each defeated enemy:

    • 50% 几率掉落梦境精华 ×150% chance: Dream Essence ×1
    • 30% 几率掉落回复道具（生命值 +1）30% chance: a pickup that restores 1 HP
    • 50% 几率掉落一种原料50% chance: one ingredient

    梦境精华的用途What Dream Essence is for

    梦境精华用来研发（解锁）饮品配方，每种饮品需要的数量见饮品。

    Dream Essence is spent to research (unlock) drink recipes; the amount for each drink is in Drinks.

    解锁新配方需要消耗一定数量的“梦境精华”。完成梦境战斗后可以获得更多“梦境精华”，记得多多收集哦!

    Unlocking each new recipe costs a certain amount of Dream Essence, which you can collect by defeating nightmares during dream adventures. Keep collecting Dream Essence to expand your menu!
