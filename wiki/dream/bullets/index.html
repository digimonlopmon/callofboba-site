    小料弹药Ammo

    梦境里的弹药就是奶茶原料，共 50 种：15 种饮料是副武器，其余 35 种是主武器。

    Dreamland ammo is made of drink ingredients: 50 kinds, 15 drinks for the sub weapon and 35 for the main weapon.

    • 解锁用到某种原料的饮品后，这种弹药才会出现在弹药台上；樱花花瓣Sakura Petal不需要解锁。An ammo appears on ammo pedestals once a drink that uses its ingredient is unlocked; 樱花花瓣Sakura Petal needs no unlock.
    • 清空一个房间后，房间里的弹药台有 50% 几率出现。When a room is cleared, its ammo pedestal appears with a 50% chance. 来自游戏代码From the game's code
    • 新存档和弹药重置后：On a new save and after an ammo reset: 主武器Main Weapon 珍珠Boba · 副武器Sub Weapon 红茶Black Tea
