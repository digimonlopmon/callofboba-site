    人物介绍

    从小时候，古先生就有着守护木薯镇的理想，他的成绩优异，但并没有去到大城市，而是选择留在小镇，为木薯镇变更好而努力，和小镇相关的事都会认真严肃处理。但古先生并不古板守旧，而是尊重大家的不同想法，因而很受爱戴。同时作为镇长，他更是了解小镇的一切，包括他人未知的秘密。

    性格

    责任感很强，严肃认真，待人真诚。很有镇长的威严，有时候说话比较严厉。

    About

    Ever since he was small, Mr. Wise has wanted to look after Tapioca Town. His grades were outstanding, but he did not leave for the big city. He stayed to work at making the town better, and he treats anything to do with it seriously. He is not stuffy or old-fashioned, though: he respects that people think differently, and he is well loved for it. As mayor he also knows everything about the town, including secrets nobody else does.

    Personality

    A strong sense of duty, serious and earnest, and sincere with people. He carries a mayor's authority, and he can be stern when he speaks.
