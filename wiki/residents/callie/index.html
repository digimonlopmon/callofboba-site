    人物介绍

    嘉仪是木薯镇上的语文老师。嘉仪从小就是“别人家的孩子”，一直走在父母规划好的路上，过着稳定平淡，一眼就可以望到尽头的生活。啵啵的出现以及他对做奶茶的热爱和执着，让她想起了曾经那个在教室里对着窗外摇晃的树叶出神的自己。在她的笔下曾经有无数个天马行空的世界，可不知何时起，她连梦都不再做了。一成不变的日子，重复无数次的课文，连游戏和电影都不再能提起她的兴趣。每当她来到避雨巷，她发现自己又燃起了写故事的兴趣。于是她决定改变。

    性格

    表面上是思想成熟、善解人意、温柔体贴的“姐姐”，实则内心有很多无厘头的想法，喜欢观察和设想。

    About

    Callie teaches Chinese at the school in Tapioca Town. She was always the model child, following the path her parents laid out toward a steady, quiet life she could see all the way to the end of. Then Bobo turned up, stubbornly in love with making milk tea, and she remembered the girl who used to stare out the classroom window at the swaying leaves. She once filled pages with wild, impossible worlds. At some point she stopped even dreaming. The days stayed the same, the same lessons came round again and again, and not even games or films could hold her interest. But whenever she comes to Sheltered Lane she finds she wants to write stories again. So she has decided to change.

    Personality

    On the surface she is the mature, understanding, gentle big sister. Underneath she is full of offbeat ideas, and she loves to watch people and imagine.
