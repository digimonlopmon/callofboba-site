    第一次开店Your first days behind the counter

    从推开店门到收工结算，头几天在避雨巷奶茶店要做的事。

    What the first few days at the Sheltered Lane Milk Tea Shop ask of you, from opening the door to counting up at closing.

    文中的按键是键盘默认键位，可以在设置里改。全部键位见操作。

    Keys named here are the keyboard defaults, and you can change them in the settings. The full list is on Controls.

    开店前Before you open

    店门每天 6:00 开，19:00 关。进店后先到的是准备间，左下角有一个收起来的小抽屉，按 E 打开，里面是四样东西：

    The shop door is open from 6:00 to 19:00. You walk into the prep room first. A small drawer sits folded away in the bottom-left corner. Press E to open it and you get four buttons:

    • 图鉴（C）：看配方，研发新饮品。Recipe (C): look up recipes and research new drinks.
    • 内饰（J）：买家具，摆桌椅。Interior (J): buy furniture and arrange tables and chairs.
    • 开店（Z）：选今天卖什么，然后开始营业。Open (Z): choose what to sell today, then start the shift.
    • 店员（K）：雇人帮忙，要等剧情解锁。Staff (K): hire help. This unlocks later in the story.

    按开店后先选今日菜单：用左右键移到一个空位，按 Z 打开饮品列表，挑一款放进去。选好以后长按 F 确认，店就开了。

    Pressing Open takes you to today's menu. Move left or right onto an empty slot, press Z to open the drink list, and pick a drink for it. When the menu is set, hold F to confirm and the shop opens.

    菜单一格都不放也能开店，这时候店里只卖原味奶茶。

    You can open with every slot empty. If you do, the shop sells nothing but plain Milk Tea.

    一天只能营业一次。收工以后，或者过了关门时间，开店按钮会变暗，要等到第二天。

    You get one shift a day. After you close, or once closing time has passed, the Open button goes dark until tomorrow.

    做一杯Making a drink

    客人在柜台点单，订单会显示在他们头顶。按配方一步一步来：

    Customers order at the counter, and the order shows above their heads. Follow the recipe one step at a time:

    1. 拿一个杯子。Take a cup.
    2. 加订单要的小料。Add the ingredients the order asks for.
    3. 加牛奶、茶这些液体。Add the liquids: milk, tea and so on.
    4. 把做好的饮品交给客人。Hand the finished drink to the customer.

    做错了也不要紧，把这杯倒进水槽，重新拿杯子再做。

    A mistake costs you little. Pour the drink out at the sink and start again with a fresh cup.

    有些饮品要用到奶泡机或者冰淇淋机，玩法见新手教程里对应的两张说明。

    Some drinks need the milk frother or the ice cream machine. The Tutorials page has the game's own cards for both.

    外带和堂食Takeout and dine-in

    客人分两种。外带的客人站在柜台前等，比较着急，做好直接递给他们；先给谁由你决定。

    There are two kinds of customer. Takeout customers wait at the counter and are in a hurry. Hand them their drink as soon as it is ready, in whatever order suits you.

    堂食的客人会找位子坐下，没那么急，但会在意店里的环境和服务。他们的饮品要送到桌边：从柜台旁边绕出去，走到桌前交给他们，再回到柜台后面做下一杯。

    Dine-in guests find a seat. They are in less of a rush, but they care how the place looks and how they are served. Their drinks go to the table: walk out around the side of the counter, hand the drink over at the table, then come back behind the counter for the next one.

    雇了店员以后，堂食的饮品可以放进左边的小推车，店员会送过去。店员忙不过来的时候，你也可以自己送。

    Once you have staff, you can put dine-in drinks in the cart on the left and they will deliver them. When they are swamped, you can still carry a drink over yourself.

    客人的耐心有限，等太久会不高兴。顾客的种类和耐心见顾客。

    Customers only have so much patience, and a long wait sours them. Customer types and patience are covered on Customers.

    收工Closing up

    到了关门时间，营业自动结束，弹出当天的结算。卖出多少杯、客人满不满意、赚了多少、收到多少小费、店里好不好看，都会算进经验。

    At closing time the shift ends by itself and the day's summary appears. Cups sold, how happy your customers were, profit, tips and how good the shop looks all count toward experience.

    升级不会弹窗提醒，只能在结算的经验条上看到。等级奖励要自己去领：打开主菜单的书，翻到等级那一页，一级一级领。配方、种子和梦境精华都在里面，别忘了。

    Levelling up gives no pop-up. You only see it on the experience bar in the summary. Level rewards have to be collected by hand: open the book in the main menu, turn to the level page, and claim them one level at a time. Recipes, seeds and Dream Essence are waiting there, so remember to look.

    每一级解锁什么，见店铺等级。

    What each level unlocks is listed on Shop levels.

    往后会多出什么What opens up later

    • 研发新饮品：拿到新配方以后，在图鉴里花梦境精华研发，再照着配方亲手调一杯，就能放进菜单。调的时候清单上写的是“少量”“适量”“大量”，倒到打勾为止；液体会慢慢沉下去，勾可能又消失，稍微多倒一点就稳了。倒多了按重置（C）重来。梦境精华从梦境里带回来，全部配方见饮品。Researching new drinks: when you get a new recipe, spend Dream Essence on it under Recipe, then mix one cup by hand to the recipe, and it can go on the menu. The checklist asks for a "small amount", a "medium amount" or a "huge amount"; pour until the tick appears. Liquids settle, so a tick can vanish again, and pouring a touch more keeps it there. If you overpour, press reset (C) and start over. Dream Essence comes back with you from Dreamland, and every recipe is on Drinks.
    • 天赋：店铺升到 2 级起，每隔几级可以三选一。见天赋。Talents: from shop level 2, every few levels you pick one of three. See Talents.
    • 夜间营业：剧情推进后店可以开到 24:00，傍晚再开店，就会遇到一些奇特的客人。Night shifts: later in the story the shop can stay open until 24:00. Open in the evening and some peculiar customers will turn up.
    • 店员：剧情解锁后可以雇镇上的熟人，各有各的本事。见店员。Staff: once the story unlocks them, you can hire familiar faces from town, each with a knack of their own. See Staff.
    • 内饰：买家具、摆桌椅，把堂食区布置起来。见家具。Interior: buy furniture and set out tables and chairs for your dine-in guests. See Furniture.
