    体力偏低时When stamina runs low

    • 低于 15：提示“你开始感觉疲倦了”Below 15: the message “You're starting to feel tired”
    • 低于 0：不能钓鱼（锄地、浇水、敲石不受限制）Below 0: you cannot fish (hoeing, watering and the pickaxe still work)
    • 降到 -20 或更低：昏倒，这一天结束-20 or lower: you pass out and the day ends
