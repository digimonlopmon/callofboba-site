    天赋Talents

    店铺升到下面这些等级时，从三个天赋中选一个。

    At each of these shop levels you pick one of three talents.

    店铺 2 级Shop level 2

    口口相传Word of Mouth

    客流量+5%

    Foot traffic +5%

    疯狂星期四Thursday Frenzy

    星期四时，客流量大幅增加

    Foot traffic surges on Thursdays (significantly increased customers on that day)

    独家服务VIP Treatment

    VIP顾客满意度+10%

    VIP customer satisfaction +10%

    店铺 5 级Shop level 5

    美学研读Aesthetic Mastery

    舒适度值永久+50点

    Comfort +50 (permanent bonus to shop comfort)

    微笑服务Service with a Smile

    所有顾客满意度+5%

    Overall customer satisfaction +5%

    付费意愿Ready to Spend

    忠诚顾客数量上限增加，获得小费的概率+5%

    Max loyal customers increased & tip chance +5%

    店铺 8 级Shop level 8

    用户调研Market Research

    在客户排队时提前知晓玩家的订单

    Allows you to see waiting customers' orders ahead of time

    外立面修缮Facade Renovation

    客流量+5%，舒适度值+100

    Foot traffic +5%, Comfort +100

    灯光设计Lighting Design

    顾客耐心值+5%

    Customer patience +5%

    店铺 11 级Shop level 11

    手工工艺Craftsmanship

    优秀的手艺使得玩家获得小费的概率+5%

    Excellent craftsmanship increases tip chance by 5%

    匠心之作Personal Touch

    玩家亲自送达，小费费用*1.5倍

    1.5× tips when the player delivers orders in person

    背景音乐Background Music

    提升顾客满意度10%，增加顾客耐心度5%

    Customer satisfaction +10%, customer patience +5%

    店铺 14 级Shop level 14

    合理换台Quick Turnover

    客人饮用饮品时间-5%

    Customer drinking time -5% (customers finish drinks faster)

    基础培训Basic Training

    员工移动速度+1级

    Staff movement speed +1 level

    冰淇淋合作大使Ice Cream Craze

    冰淇淋客户概率+5%

    Chance of ice cream customers +5%

    店铺 17 级Shop level 17

    高级培训Advanced Training

    员工单次可携带的杯数上限永久+1

    Staff can carry 1 more cup at once (permanently)

    喇叭轰炸Megaphone Blast

    客流量+10%

    Foot traffic +10%

    科创评测区UP主Trendsetter

    当日使用四级及以上饮品菜谱时，高质量顾客吸引概率+3%

    High-end customer attraction chance +3% when using Level 4+ drink recipes

    店铺 20 级Shop level 20

    漂移调饮Quick Roll

    玩家解锁经营模式状态中的翻滚功能

    Unlocks a roll (dodge) action in Management Mode

    深夜食堂Midnight Diner

    最晚营业时间延长至凌晨1:00

    Closing time extended to 1:00 AM

    健步如飞Swift Stride

    玩家在店内的移动速度+5%

    Player movement speed in store +5%
