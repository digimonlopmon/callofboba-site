    顾客Customers

    外带与堂食Takeout & dine-in

    奶茶店中的订单分为两种，第一种叫做“外带订单”:客人们会遇到偶尔赶时间或者想路上边走边喝的情况。这部分客人的订单往往比较着急。

    The orders in the milk tea shop are divided into two types. The first type is called "takeout orders": customers may be in a hurry or want to drink while walking. These customers' orders are often urgent.

    第二种订单叫做“堂食订单”:这部分客人们享受在店里慢慢品尝饮品的时光，这部分客人的订单不太着急，但往往会对店面的环境和服务有所要求。

    The second type of order is called "dine-in orders": these customers enjoy the leisurely time to taste their drinks in the shop. These customers' orders are not very urgent, but they often have certain requirements for the environment and service of the store.

    外带顾客排队等待，耐心为 15–102 秒；堂食顾客入座后等待，耐心为 30–152 秒。店内舒适度会再加上舒适度的平方根（秒）。

    Takeout customers wait in the queue with 15–102 s of patience; dine-in customers wait after sitting down, with 30–152 s. The square root of the Comfort Level is added (in seconds).

    出餐评价Order ratings

    每单按送达时顾客剩余的耐心评价；店内舒适度越高，各档门槛越低。

    Each order is rated by how much of the customer's patience is left when it is served; a higher Comfort Level lowers each threshold.

    当日满意度评分Daily satisfaction rating

    营业结束时按当天每单的平均满意度给出 1–5 级评分；店铺等级每升 1 级，各档门槛提高 0.05。

    At the end of business the day's average satisfaction per order gives a rating of 1 to 5; each shop level raises every threshold by 0.05.
