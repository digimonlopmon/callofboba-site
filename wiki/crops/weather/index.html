    天气与季节Weather & Seasons

    天气Weather

    每晚按下面的几率决定第二天的天气。

    Each night, the next day's weather is rolled with these odds.

    季节Season晴Sunny多云Cloudy刮风Windy雨Rain雪Snow
    春Spring34%14.8%33.1%18.2%—
    夏Summer37.8–42%38.6–42.9%—15.2–23.6%—
    秋Fall39.7%17.3%24.8%18.2%—
    冬Winter27.5%11.9%——60.6%

    夏季的雨天几率随日期上升；前一天下过雨时取最低值。

    In summer the chance of rain rises with the date; after a rainy day it is at its lowest.
