Come home to Tapioca Town

Bobo worked himself into a hospital bed in the big city. He quit his job and caught the bus back to his hometown, Tapioca Town.

He's barely off the bus when a tricycle sends him sprawling. The rider is Stellar, a childhood friend he hasn't seen in years. Stellar's grandpa left him a decades-old milk tea shop with cracking floorboards and books deep in the red. Everyone tells him to let it go.

So Bobo stays.

What you do

  • Mix it yourself

    Milk teas, foam teas, fruit teas, coffees. Over 50 recipes, and every cup is made by your own hands.

  • Open the doors

    Takeout customers are in a hurry. Dine-in guests care how the place looks. Set the tables, hire familiar faces from town, and bring Sheltered Lane back to life.

  • Fall asleep, fall into a dream

    Printers, security cameras, Mr. Stamp. Everything Bobo used to dread is waiting in Dreamland. Fire pearls, red beans, and tea right back at them, and bring home ingredients. Not tonight? Switch on the lamp and sleep till morning.

  • Fish to the beat

    Every fish is one part of a song. Hook a Fish King and the whole band plays.

  • From the field to the cup

    31 crops across spring, summer, and fall. Grind and simmer your harvest into tomorrow's toppings.

  • Life in town

    18 townsfolk keep their own routines. Learn their favorite gifts, and don't forget their birthdays.

Where we're at

Call of Boba is in Early Access on Steam, and it isn't finished. The main story hasn't reached its ending, and there's no romance or festivals yet.

We're Tomatoast, a core team of four. With so few hands, our pace is uneven. Every update is written up in our Steam news.
